**ARC Creative Media — Brand Identity Description**

A bold and modern visual identity built around ARC Creative Media’s signature electric-blue and white palette. The branding combines a distinctive ARC logo with clean typography, digital marketing messaging, and a dynamic curved graphic language.

The identity is applied consistently across **business cards, stationery, mugs, office signage, mobile screens, apparel, notebooks, and digital interfaces**, creating a cohesive and professional brand presence.

**Brand feel:** Modern • Creative • Digital • Bold • Professional • Growth-focused
